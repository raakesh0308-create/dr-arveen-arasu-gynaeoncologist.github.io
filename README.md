# Dr Arveen Arasu — Gynecologic Oncology Notes

A growing educational website containing notes and resources related to gynecologic oncology.

## Purpose

This site is intended to organize learning notes, clinical topics, and reference material in gynecologic oncology.

## Topics

- Ovarian cancer
- Cervical cancer
- Endometrial cancer
- Vulvar and vaginal cancers
- Diagnosis and staging
- Treatment approaches
- Follow-up and survivorship
- Research and educational resources

## Important notice

This website is for educational and informational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Do not include patient names, dates of birth, medical-record numbers, photographs, or any other identifying or confidential patient information on this public website.

Always seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical concerns.
